Privacy Policy

Hala Ai ("Hala", "we", "us"). Effective 22 October 2026.

1. What Hala does

Hala is an assistant that customers of a studio talk to on WhatsApp. It answers questions about the studio's classes and appointments and, when you ask, books, changes or cancels them in the studio's booking system. It can also send you confirmations and reminders about your bookings.

2. Information we collect

  • From WhatsApp: your WhatsApp phone number or WhatsApp user ID, your WhatsApp profile name, the messages you send us, and the time and delivery status of messages.
  • From the studio's booking system (Mindbody): your client record at the studio (such as your name, email and phone number), your upcoming bookings, and the bookings you make or change through Hala.
  • Technical records: logs we need to run and secure the service.

3. How we use it

  • To understand your messages and reply to them.
  • To find your booking record and to make, change or cancel bookings you ask for.
  • To send confirmations and reminders about your bookings.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. AI processing

Hala uses artificial intelligence language models to understand your messages and write replies. The text of your messages, and the studio information needed to answer them, is sent to the AI service providers we use, which process it on our behalf to produce the reply.

5. Who we share information with

  • The studio you are messaging, which is responsible for its own customer records.
  • Mindbody, the studio's booking system, to look up and change bookings.
  • Meta (WhatsApp), which carries the messages.
  • AI service providers and hosting providers that process data for us to run the service.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own.

6. How long we keep it

We keep conversation records for as long as we need them to provide the service to you and the studio, or until you ask us to delete them. See Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Security

We protect information with access controls and encryption in transit, and we limit who can see it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to amoiz.09x@gmail.com; we aim to reply within 30 days. Information the studio holds in its own booking system is controlled by the studio, so contact the studio about that.

9. Children

Hala is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
