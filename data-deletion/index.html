Data Deletion Instructions

You can ask Hala Ai to delete the personal information we hold about you from our systems.

How to ask

  1. Send an email to amoiz.09x@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number you used to message the studio, and the name of the studio.
  3. We may ask you to confirm that the number is yours, for example by messaging the studio's WhatsApp number from it.

What happens next

We aim to delete your information and confirm by email within 30 days. We delete your contact record, your conversation history and your message records from our systems.

What we cannot delete for you

  • Your client record and bookings in the studio's own booking system (Mindbody) belong to the studio. Please contact the studio to have those removed.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) keeps its own copies under its own policies.
  • Backups are overwritten on their normal schedule.

See also our Privacy Policy.
